Guidelines

Who Can Apply

  • Students enrolled in a Nigerian university, polytechnic or college of education
  • All levels — ND, HND and 100 to 600 level
  • Applicants from all 36 states and the FCT

What You Need

  • Email address and password for your account
  • JAMB admission letter
  • Last school fees receipt
  • NIN, date of birth, state of origin and LGA

Verification & Payout

  • Face liveness check
  • ₦3,000 verification fee — Paystack
  • Payout to Grant Wallet or bank account

FAQs

Is the last school fees receipt compulsory?
Yes. Required.
Which institutions are covered?
Universities, polytechnics and colleges of education — ND to 600 level.
What is the liveness check?
On-device face scan: centre, turn, tilt, blink.
How do I pay the ₦3,000 verification fee?
Paystack — card, bank transfer, USSD or bank account.
How do I receive the grant after verification?
Grant Wallet or bank account.
Where is my account saved?
In this browser only.
Is this the live application portal?
No. Competition prototype.

Presentation notice: This website was built for an internal design competition and is a prototype for presentation only. It is not an official Federal Government of Nigeria website and it is not connected to any government programme. Demo accounts are stored only in your own browser, with passwords kept as secure hashes — nothing is transmitted to a server, and applications, NINs, documents and bank details are not stored. The ₦3,000 verification fee is shown through Paystack in test mode and all payout screens are simulated for demonstration — no real charge is made.
