About the Program

The Grant Program is a flagship educational initiative of the Federal government administration, designed to provide direct financial support to students of every federal Government Students In Nigeria studying in public tertiary institutions across Nigeria. This program reflects the government's unwavering commitment to making quality education accessible and affordable for every student in Nigeria.

₦50,000 Grant per student

Guidelines

Who Can Apply

  • Students enrolled in a Nigerian university, polytechnic or college of education
  • All levels — ND, HND and 100 to 600 level
  • Applicants from all 36 states and the FCT
  • Grant amount: ₦50,000 per student

What You Need

  • Email address and password for your account
  • JAMB admission letter
  • Last school fees receipt
  • NIN, date of birth, state of origin and LGA
  • Matric number and CGPA of 3.50 or above
  • Statement of Result and student ID card
  • Certificate of Origin, birth certificate and passport photograph
  • Phone number and parents' occupation / means of livelihood

Verification & Payout

  • Face liveness check
  • ₦3,000 verification fee — bank transfer
  • ₦50,000 payout to your Grant Wallet

FAQs

How much is the grant?
The grant is ₦50,000 per student. After your application is approved, the ₦50,000 is paid into your Grant Wallet.
Is the last school fees receipt compulsory?
Yes, it is compulsory. You upload it at the Documents & identity step, together with your JAMB admission letter, and the application will not move forward without it. It should be the receipt for your most recent school fees payment at your institution, uploaded as a PDF or a clear photo or scan of the receipt.
Which institutions are covered?
The grant covers students in universities, polytechnics and colleges of education across all 36 states and the FCT. All levels are eligible, from ND and HND through to 100 level to 600 level. During the application you select your institution type, institution name, department and level from the lists provided.
What is the liveness check?
The liveness check is a short face scan that confirms you are a real, live person and not a photo or someone else applying in your name. At the Documents & identity step, tap Face Scan / Liveness Check to open the full-screen scan, then follow the instructions shown on the camera view: centre your face in the frame, turn and tilt your head as directed, and blink when asked. Once every step is completed, the scan is marked as confirmed and you can continue.
How do I pay the ₦3,000 verification fee?
The ₦3,000 is a one-time verification fee, paid at the Verification fee step through Paystack. You can pay by card, bank transfer, USSD or bank account, with bank transfer shown as the recommended option. Your email address and payment reference are shown on that step before you pay. Once the payment goes through, your application shows as Pending — Waiting for Approval.
How do I receive the grant after verification?
The ₦50,000 grant is paid into your FGSG Grant Wallet by default — there is no payout choice to make in the application. You can see your balance and status in the Bank section of your dashboard, and withdraw from the wallet to your bank account there whenever you are ready. After you submit, your application shows as Pending — Waiting for Approval, with your application reference.
How do I withdraw the grant to my bank?
After approval, when the ₦50,000 is in your Grant Wallet, open Bank and tap Withdraw Grant. Select your commercial bank — mobile money banks are not accepted — and enter your 10-digit account number. Your account is checked and validated through Paystack, and your account name is shown. Tap Continue, type the amount you want to withdraw or tap Max for the full remaining balance, then confirm — that amount leaves your Grant Wallet and the withdrawal shows as Queued. You do not have to withdraw everything at once: withdraw ₦25,000 and the other ₦25,000 stays in your wallet, ready for another withdrawal whenever you want.
Where is my transaction history?
Open Bank and scroll to Transaction History. It shows every movement in order: the ₦3,000 verification fee you sent for processing, the ₦50,000 grant received into your Grant Wallet, and every withdrawal you make, each one marked Queued. Tap any transaction to open its receipt, with the amount, date, reference, bank details and balance after it.
Can I cancel and restart my application?
Yes — while you are still filling in the application, on any step before you submit, you can tap Cancel & Restart to clear everything and start again from the beginning. Once you tap Submit Application at the final step, the application is locked: you cannot cancel it, restart it, or submit a second application.
